NearbyNook Support

NearbyNook helps you save, organize, and revisit the places that matter to you.

How do I save a place?

Tap the + button, search for a location or drop a pin on the map, then add notes, photos, and a category before saving.

How do nearby notifications work?

When you enable location access, NearbyNook can alert you when you're close to a place you've saved. You can turn this on or off in the app settings or in iPhone Settings → NearbyNook.

How do I organize my saved places?

You can create custom categories from the main screen and assign any saved place to one or more categories.

How do I navigate to a saved place?

Tap any saved place and select the Navigate button to open directions in Maps.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app → Apple ID → Subscriptions → NearbyNook. Apple handles all billing — we have no ability to process refunds directly.

My photos aren't saving — what do I do?

Make sure NearbyNook has permission to access your photos. Go to Settings → NearbyNook → Photos and set it to "Add Photos Only" or "Full Access."

Contact Us

Have a question or found a bug? Email us at nearbynook@morbertechnologies.com. We typically respond within 1–2 business days.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Morber Technologies LLC ("we," "us," or "our") built NearbyNook as a personal place-saving app. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Location Data
NearbyNook uses your device's location to show nearby saved places and to send proximity notifications. Location is used on-device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can revoke location access at any time in Settings → NearbyNook → Location.

Photos
If you choose to attach photos to a saved place, those photos are stored locally on your device. We do not upload or store your photos on external servers.

Notes
Any notes you write are stored locally on your device and are not shared with us or any third party.

Purchase Information
NearbyNook offers an optional subscription. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information.

How We Use Your Information

We use information solely to provide core app functionality — saving places, navigation, and notifications — and to improve app performance. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

NearbyNook uses Google Maps for map display and navigation. Google's privacy policy applies to their services. Subscription purchases are handled by Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to App Store transactions.

Data Storage

Your saved places, notes, and photos are stored locally on your device. If you use iCloud Backup, this data may be included per Apple's iCloud terms.

Children's Privacy

NearbyNook is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will post changes on this page with an updated date.
