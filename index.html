NearbyNook Support

NearbyNook helps you save, organize, and revisit the places that matter to you.

How do I save a place?

Tap the + button, search for a location or drop a pin on the map, then add notes, photos, and a category before saving.

How do nearby notifications work?

When you enable location access, NearbyNook can alert you when you're close to a place you've saved. You can turn this on or off in the app settings or in iPhone Settings → NearbyNook.

How do I organize my saved places?

You can create custom categories from the main screen and assign any saved place to one or more categories.

How do I navigate to a saved place?

Tap any saved place and select the Navigate button to open directions in Maps.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app → Apple ID → Subscriptions → NearbyNook. Apple handles all billing — we have no ability to process refunds directly.

My photos aren't saving — what do I do?

Make sure NearbyNook has permission to access your photos. Go to Settings → NearbyNook → Photos and set it to "Add Photos Only" or "Full Access."
